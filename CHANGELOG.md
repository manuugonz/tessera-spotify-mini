# Changelog

## 0.1.0
- Native player with a three-line menu for search, recent songs, suggested playlists and library access.
- Device selection at the player's top left, larger artwork and spaced playback controls; no on-screen volume.
- Cover-first portrait tiles, split wide tiles and responsive monochrome player controls.
- Separate Home Assistant OAuth backend.
- TECHO5 needs the documented adapter; hardware validation is pending.
